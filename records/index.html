Element records

The extremes of the periodic table, worked out from measured values. Elements made only a few atoms at a time have no measured values and are left out.

  5. Nitrogen: 14.534 eV
  6. Krypton: 14 eV

Liquid at room temperature

Only two elements: bromine, mercury. Caesium, gallium and rubidium melt a little above room temperature, between 28 and 40 °C.

Gases at room temperature

11 elements: hydrogen, helium, nitrogen, oxygen, fluorine, neon, chlorine, argon, krypton, xenon, radon.

Known since ancient times

11 elements have no recorded discoverer: carbon, sulfur, iron, copper, arsenic, silver, tin, antimony, gold, mercury, lead.

Most recently discovered

The six newest: tennessine (2010), oganesson (2006), nihonium (2004), moscovium (2003), livermorium (2000), flerovium (1998). All were made in particle accelerators.
